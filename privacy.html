Gesture Booth - Privacy Policy Last updated: November 15, 2026 Gesture Booth is a personal hobby project (a Raspberry Pi photo booth) run by one person for private use. It is not a commercial service. What the app accesses The app connects to the owner's own Google Drive using the limited "drive.file" permission. With that permission it can only see and manage files and folders that the app itself created. It cannot read any other files in the Drive. What it stores Finished photos taken at the booth are saved to a folder named "Gesture Booth" in the owner's Google Drive. A photo may be shared by a link, which is shown to the person in the photo as a QR code. The booth saves two pictures per visit: the finished framed picture and the original camera photo. The QR code opens a small page on this site that shows those two pictures. Anyone with that link can view those two pictures. What it does not do It does not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone. It does not use data for advertising. It does not send data to any server other than Google Drive. Camera frames are processed on the Raspberry Pi itself. Gesture recognition runs locally. Removing your picture If you were photographed at the booth and want your picture removed, contact the owner and it will be deleted from Google Drive, which also disables its link.
