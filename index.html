Gesture Booth

What do you want to say to the World?

Gesture Booth is a personal hobby project: a camera photo booth built on a Raspberry Pi that recognises a hand gesture, takes a photo, and places it in a matching decorative frame for a lighted top hat made of LED panels.

After a photo is taken, the booth can save the finished picture to the owner's own Google Drive and show a QR code so the person in the photo can open their picture.

This app is for the owner's private use only. It is not offered to the public.
